How windows are scored

Tide timing
Windows center on the wind-adjusted high and low tides. Each 15-minute slot scores highest at the turn, eases off slightly out to the hours you picked on either side, then drops away over the next hour.

Water level
Highs and lows are the wind-adjusted ones. Flood-tide fishing needs the adjusted high over about 5.8 ft to push water into the spartina; a blown-out low exposes more bars and mud.

Light and wind
The 75 minutes around sunrise and sunset get a boost. Slots over your wind limit drop out, and dark hours drop unless you include them.

Rating
Prime is a window averaging 75+, Good is 60+. Past the 7-day wind forecast, only the current sea-level offset is applied.
